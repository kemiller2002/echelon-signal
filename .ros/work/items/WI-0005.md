## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Keep personal identity outside survey answer payloads and support administrator-owned person-to-instance mapping for identified surveys.
- [ ] Represent explicit identity modes, respondent/subject/role semantics, group requirements, counts, dependencies, and completion.
- [ ] Finalize anonymous submissions by generating an unpredictable unlinkable AnonymousSubmissionId and removing all invitation linkage, including SurveyInstanceId.
- [ ] Preserve SurveyGroupId for aggregation while preventing person/invitation linkage and documenting the inherent person-level duplicate limitation.
- [ ] Enforce anonymous-safe result/report boundaries and minimum-count privacy constraints.
