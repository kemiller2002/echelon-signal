## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create requirement-linked unit, integration, property, characterization, privacy, security, accessibility, and end-to-end acceptance suites.
- [ ] Measure realistic response/admin URL budgets and performance across supported browsers, devices, email/messaging, copy/paste, and planned QR workflows.
- [ ] Verify round-trip encoding, canonical hashes, deterministic results/publication/rendering, wrong-template/corruption/version failures, cycle detection, and explainability.
- [ ] Threat-model untrusted template/rule/submission data, integrity versus authenticity, external-store references, PII leakage, and anonymous unlinkability.
- [ ] Preserve existing survey semantics through old/new characterization fixtures unless a separately authorized Decision Record approves a change.
