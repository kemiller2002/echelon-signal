# WI-0017: Administrator privacy, security, audit, retention, and deletion lifecycle

Status: captured  
Source authority: `input-documents/survey-engine-administrator-console-storage-analytics-visualization-requirements.txt`  
Migration ledger: `docs/requirements/survey-engine-requirements-migration.md`

## Objective

Make privacy and lifecycle behavior explicit from aggregate disclosure through archival/deletion, while producing useful audit evidence without introducing identity or secrets.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Evaluate privacy/suppression before unauthorized rows/cells reach reporting or visualization projections.
- [ ] Detect/block supported differencing attacks and enforce thresholds after interactive filtering as well as static reporting.
- [ ] Define structured audit events free of PII, secrets, raw URLs, and unnecessary answer content.
- [ ] Model retention/archive/retirement/deletion states and accurately represent GitHub history limitations rather than claiming irreversible deletion.
- [ ] Ensure undo, policy packs, exports, snapshots, and migrations preserve privacy/audit/lifecycle invariants.
