ECHELON SIGNAL
ASSESSMENT BATTERY REQUIREMENTS DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

Status: Discovery artifact
Added: 2026-09-22

Purpose
=======

This document is a comprehensive question set for defining first-class assessment-battery support in Echelon Signal.

An assessment battery is intentionally treated as potentially more than the existing SurveyGroup concept. A battery may coordinate multiple immutable assessments/instruments under shared sequencing, adaptive selection, timing, breaks, repeated administration, scoring, norms, interpretation, privacy, accessibility, and reporting rules.

These questions are NOT answers and are NOT yet normative product requirements. Each question should eventually be answered, explicitly deferred, or declared out of scope. Material answers should then be converted into numbered authoritative battery requirements and ROS work-item traceability.

Existing Signal constraints continue to apply while these questions are answered, including:
- no PII in Signal,
- F# as application/domain authority,
- Ordo/SDE legal-state/capability/obligation semantics,
- Limen for browser mechanism boundaries,
- Aegis for unexpected operational failures,
- deterministic/versioned/hash-addressed semantics where formal reproducibility is required,
- explicit unknown effects and reconciliation,
- accessibility and privacy as acceptance properties rather than presentation afterthoughts.

Question IDs are stable discovery identifiers.
